Clarity privacy policy

Published: September 28, 2026. Last updated: October 2, 2026.

Rollout status: The updated privacy controls described below have been implemented and tested but have not yet been deployed to the live bot. The automated commands, bounded chat history, opt-out enforcement and new AI-request settings describe the upcoming release, not a guarantee about the version currently running. Until deployment is confirmed here, request opt-out or deletion through #data-opt-out-requests. Older versions may retain chat logs, personal memories and provider-side conversation history as described below. Publishing this notice does not deploy those controls or confirm completion of the storage and provider checks.

Clarity is a community support assistant used in participating Discord servers. Privacy support is available in Clarity's Discord server. This policy describes how Clarity's operator handles Discord information. Discord and OpenAI also have their own privacy policies and terms.

Contact and requests

Contact Clarity's operator through #data-opt-out-requests in Clarity's Discord server. Do not post passwords, API keys, private message contents, or sensitive personal information there. If confidential follow-up is needed, ask the operator for a private route.

Once the updated release is deployed and Clarity is online, these commands provide a private response:

  • /privacy info: policy and contact information.
  • /privacy opt-out: exclude your account from new conversational and FAQ processing.
  • /privacy delete-request: opt out and submit a tracked deletion request.
  • /privacy status: check your exclusion and deletion-request status.

You can also request correction or deletion through the contact channel. The operator must verify that a request concerns your own account. Requests are not sent to an AI model. A request receipt means the request was received, not that every copy has already been deleted.

What Clarity processes

When you mention Clarity in an allowed channel, Clarity processes the message, relevant Discord identifiers and display name, and supplied mod information to prepare a response. Replies remain in Discord according to Discord's behavior and server permissions.

When support analytics is enabled, Clarity retrieves messages from configured mod-support channels and configured bug-report forum threads. Relevant records include message text, message/channel/author identifiers, timestamps, membership/bot flags, and topic classifications. In the updated release, usernames and full role-name lists are not retained in new FAQ source records; roles are used transiently for support filtering. Older records may still contain those fields until cleanup. Author identifiers are retained to honor opt-out and deletion requests. This data is not anonymous: identifiers and message text may identify individuals. Messages are grouped by mod and support topic to help administrators understand recurring issues. Noise content is filtered out of retained FAQ records.

Clarity also retains factual mod corrections submitted by the specifically authorized mod author. Optional administrator tools may retain job-search results, command-completion records and notification metadata. These are separate from the community FAQ dataset.

Clarity keeps minimal operational information needed to run the service, plus your Discord account identifier and timestamps when necessary to honor an opt-out or track a deletion request.

AI processing and sharing

Clarity sends conversational requests and selected support-message examples to the OpenAI API to generate replies and summaries. FAQ samples receive automated filtering for some identifiers, email addresses and similar strings; this is not a guarantee of anonymization. Do not submit sensitive personal information.

Clarity does not train or fine-tune models using Discord messages. The operator must keep optional provider data-sharing for training disabled. In the updated release, chat and FAQ requests use store=false and do not create OpenAI Conversations. OpenAI may still retain data for abuse monitoring or other purposes described in its API data controls.

The operator and authorized administrators can access data required for support and maintenance. Discord receives replies, and OpenAI receives the inputs described above. Support summaries requested through Discord commands can be visible to people who can read the response channel. Clarity does not sell Discord message data or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers.

Retention and older records

In the updated release, Clarity stores up to six completed mention/reply exchanges per user and channel in local SQLite, linked to your Discord user ID and channel ID. Each side is limited to 4,000 characters; up to 12,000 characters of recent context may be sent with the next AI request. Context expires after 30 days and is pruned at startup and on context reads/writes; if the application is stopped, physical cleanup happens when it next starts. Opt-out and deletion requests immediately remove these new local context records. No automatic personal-fact profiles or separate chat-transcript files are created. Historical personal memories and stored OpenAI Conversations are not reused.

The FAQ collector excludes and prunes source messages older than six months when collection runs. Analytics keeps at most ten saved runs and twenty saved AI summaries. These count limits are not fixed age limits; the operator must also remove information that is no longer needed. Opt-outs invalidate stored derived FAQ results and future analyses exclude the opted-out author's attributable source records.

Earlier Clarity versions retained local transcripts, conversation turns, personal memories and OpenAI Conversations. They may still exist until operator cleanup is completed. Turning on this version does not automatically erase those historical files, remote records or backups. They are included in deletion-request review.

Authoritative mod corrections have no automatic age expiry and are kept while needed for mod support, subject to correction and deletion review. The operator reviews optional command/notification records and backups separately. Minimal exclusion records are kept while needed to prevent recollection; deletion-request records document receipt and completion.

Opt-out and deletion

In the updated release, an opt-out prevents Clarity from using your newly received messages for its conversational and FAQ workflows. Discord may still deliver message events or channel-history responses to the bot; opted-out messages are skipped. Requests already sent to a provider cannot be recalled, but the app discards returned content when it detects the privacy change. Someone else may quote information about you; contact the operator if such information needs review.

In the updated release, a deletion request immediately removes new local conversation context, directly attributable local FAQ source records and personal durable-memory rows, invalidates derived FAQ results, and retains an exclusion record. Shared historical transcripts, unattributed legacy records, authoritative corrections, provider records, previously posted Discord summaries, notification records and backups require operator review. The request remains pending until that cleanup is verified. Unrelated users' information is not automatically erased to satisfy a request.

Clarity does not delete your original Discord posts through this workflow. You can manage those through Discord and the server's moderation process. The operator handles applicable correction/deletion requests promptly and communicates any remaining limitations before marking a request complete.

Safeguards and changes

Before enabling processing, the operator must verify encrypted storage for the database, logs, temporary storage and backups, restrict access, publish this policy, and review provider settings. The updated release offers explicit Windows EFS setup on compatible Windows editions (not Windows Home) for application-home files and log folders, plus verification checks; it has not yet been activated on live storage. External copies, backups and system storage need separate protection and recovery arrangements. Clarity's setup flags record operator verification; they do not encrypt a disk themselves. The operator must keep those protections in place and respond to data-security incidents.

Material changes to collection, sharing or retention will be reflected in this policy and communicated in the server before the changed processing is enabled.
