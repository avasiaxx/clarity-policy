What Clarity does

Clarity can answer supported mod questions when mentioned in an allowed channel. When enabled, its FAQ analytics can analyze messages in specifically configured support channels and bug-report threads to identify recurring issues and provide administrator summaries. Some features may be paused or limited while the operator completes privacy, security, or platform review. Clarity may also offer other clearly labeled administrator tools; their availability depends on server configuration.

Clarity uses AI to generate some replies and summaries. They can be incomplete or wrong. Check important technical instructions against official mod documentation and a human administrator before acting on them. Clarity does not provide emergency, medical, legal, or financial advice.
